# End of line

A Fluid release. Everything in it came from using 0.18.0 in Safari against a live team domain for a week, plus the things you wanted right after.

## New & Noteworthy

- **The domain page and the folder page are two pages now.** Click the domain in the sidebar and you get the domain: sync, proposals, members, review mode, the MANIFEST and every engram. Click a folder and you get the folder: its path up top ("project2030 / explorations", each step a link), how many engrams live under it, and the engrams. No archive buttons, no unregister, no manifest on a folder page. They never belonged there. Members and review mode moved to the bottom of the domain page: set once, read rarely.
- **The MANIFEST shows its features, not just its text.** The domain page reads the manifest the way an agent does and lays it out: what routes an agent here (When to Use, or Scope as the fallback), what the domain provisions, which tags fold into which, and the configuration switches. Bullets that didn't parse are listed with the reason. The whole MANIFEST is rendered underneath, with an Edit action for administrators. One source of truth: the file.
- **Switch domains from the sidebar.** The dropdown is gone. The domain's name sits at the top of the navigation as a link to its page, and the arrows beside it open the domain list.
- **Pick the order of an engram list.** Newest first, oldest first, by name A to Z or Z to A. It's remembered in your browser and applies to every domain and folder listing. Default: newest first, by the date they were recorded, so a domain page opens on what's new.
- **Images get the full window.** Any image in an engram now has the same corner toolbar a diagram has and opens in the overlay with pan and zoom. Click the image, or the button.
- **Download and copy from the full window.** A diagram downloads as its source (.mmd) or as SVG, an image downloads as itself, and a diagram's source copies to the clipboard with one click for pasting into another engram. Diagram files are named after the engram plus a short hash, so two diagrams from one document never overwrite each other.
- **Large text for presenting.** One toggle beside the width and theme controls (also while editing) grows the document to 1.5x, three Safari zoom steps, without touching the browser zoom that used to clip diagram labels. Diagrams keep their size: pan and zoom cover those.
- **"End of line."** Scroll an engram list to its last page and Fluid signs off the way a certain 1982 mainframe did. Only where you scrolled to reach it. Never in the sidebar.

## Fixes

- A big diagram now fits its column and "Show at full width" actually widens it. Before, both states drew the same picture for exactly the diagrams the button was for. Behaviour change: a diagram fits by default now; the toggle takes it to the room there is.
- Full width uses the whole window. The reading frame kept a cap at full width, so wide screens still had margins.
- The share badge counted work you had already proposed. With an open stacked proposal the header said "2 changes to share" while the dialog said there was nothing. Both count against the chain tip now, on the CLI status line too.
- Graph nodes stand apart. Both graph views placed labels on top of each other; the closest pair is now at least twice as far apart.
- The copy-address control is in the editor's header too, so you can hand an agent the address of the engram you're editing without leaving it.
- On Postgres an engram without a recorded date sorted first in the domain listing and in recent activity instead of last. Both backends agree now.

## Breaking changes

None.
